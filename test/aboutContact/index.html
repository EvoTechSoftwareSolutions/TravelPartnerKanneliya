Kanneliya Rainforest is a protected natural reserve in southern Sri Lanka.

Kanneliya Rainforest is one of the last remaining lowland rainforests in Sri Lanka, offering visitors a chance to experience untouched nature and rich biodiversity. Visitors can explore scenic forest trails, observe rare plant species, and enjoy the calming atmosphere created by towering trees and flowing streams. Kanneliya plays a vital role in environmental conservation and sustainable eco tourism for future generations.

This rainforest is home to many endemic birds, reptiles, amphibians, and medicinal plants that are found nowhere else in the world. Guided nature walks provide educational experiences while promoting responsible tourism and environmental awareness. A visit to Kanneliya offers adventure, relaxation, and a deeper connection with Sri Lanka’s natural heritage.

Kanneliya Rainforest attracts nature lovers, photographers, and researchers seeking natural beauty and biodiversity.
